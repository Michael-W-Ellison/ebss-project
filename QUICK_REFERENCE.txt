CORE CONCEPTS
=============
Drives: Internal motivations that accumulate and trigger goals
Trees: Behavior trees that learn via weight adjustment
Inheritance: Offspring clone parent trees with pruning + mutation
Learning: Weights increase on success (×1.1), decrease on failure (×0.9)
Urgency: drive.value × drive.weight determines priority
Fitness: success_count / execution_count
