Durability Levels:

75%-100% (100% output/capability)
50%-74% (75% output/capability)
25%-49% (50% output/capability)
1%-24% (25% output/capability)
0% (object destroyed)
A tool with lower durability should result in less resource output (e.g., a flint axe with 60% durability produces 75% of a resource in the same amount of time as a flint axe with 80% durability produces 100% of a resources).

Clothing with lower durability starts becoming less effective at protecting the wearer against the elements.
